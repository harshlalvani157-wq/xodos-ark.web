HOW TO DISABLE THE PHANTOM PROCESSER KILLER ?

Disable child process restrictions

BUT THIS IS ONLY FOR THE ANDROID 14 && 14+

Go to your Phone settings and follow below steps :

Settings → About Phone → tap Build Number 7× → Developer Options → "Disable child process restrictions." Android 12+ kills an app's spawned processes past a threshold, and installing packages spawns one per operation — without this toggle, installs can die silently partway through.
